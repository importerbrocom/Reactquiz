How to Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your ERO account and all associated data, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to importerbro.com@gmail.com
  2. Subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include the email address associated with your ERO account
  4. We will process your request within 30 days

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email)
  • Quiz progress and scores
  • All answer history
  • Streak and level data

Note: Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.
