Ques No: 2, QuesID: 799623
Subject: Pharmacology
Topic: Autonomic Nervous System
Sub-Topic: Beta blockers

Which beta blocker is the most cardioselective and therefore preferred
in a patient with coexisting bronchial asthma
O1:
Propranolol
O2:
Nebivolol
O3:
Timolol
O4:
Pindolol
Ans: 2
Explanation: Nebivolol has the highest beta-1 selectivity of the listed agents and
additionally releases nitric oxide, so it is preferred where beta-2 blockade would
provoke bronchospasm. Propranolol and timolol are non-selective.
